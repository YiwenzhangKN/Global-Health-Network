Global disease network (2019)

Causes of death are linked if they are high in the same countries, after removing the effect of national wealth (GDP per capita). Health-system and risk-factor indicators are linked to the causes they are associated with. Hover a node for details, click it to list its links, drag to move, scroll to zoom.
